BATMAN

Batman is a legendary DC superhero known for his intelligence, detective skills, martial arts, and advanced gadgets,After losing his parents as a child, Bruce dedicates his life to fighting crime and protecting his city.He wears a dark armored suit with a bat symbol, cape, cowl, and pointed ears. Unlike Superman or Hulk, Batman has no superhuman powers. He relies on physical training, intelligence, strategy, and technology
