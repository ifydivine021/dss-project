The Five Heros: Shadows Over Metropolis

Hulk is a powerful green superhero known for his incredible strength and unstoppable energy. Spider-Man is a clever and agile hero who can climb walls, sense danger, and swing through the city with webs. Superman is a powerful hero from Krypton who can fly, move at incredible speed, and protect people from danger. Iron Man is a brilliant inventor who uses his advanced red-and-gold armor to fight evil. Batman is a skilled detective and fighter who uses intelligence, gadgets, and courage to protect Gotham City. Together, these superheroes represent strength, bravery, intelligence, responsibility, and determination. They inspire people by using their unique abilities to stand against danger and help others.
